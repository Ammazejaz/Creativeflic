Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

CreativeFlic ("we", "us") runs creativeflic.com. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you request a free audit we collect your name, work email, website or YouTube channel, revenue range, and anything you write in the message box.
  • Basic usage data. If analytics is switched on, Google Analytics records anonymised information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type and how you found the site. IP addresses are anonymised.

How we use it

  • To prepare and send the audit you asked for, and to reply to you.
  • To follow up about our services if you asked for an audit. You can tell us to stop at any time.
  • To understand which pages and videos are useful, so we can improve the site.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list without asking.

Services we use

  • Google (Sheets and Apps Script) stores audit requests.
  • Google Analytics measures site usage, when enabled.
  • YouTube plays our sample videos. Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play.
  • Google Fonts and our hosting provider, Hostinger, serve the site itself.

How long we keep it

We keep audit requests for up to 24 months, then delete them, unless you become a client, in which case we keep what we need to run the work.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Contact

To make a request, reply to any email we have sent you, or message us on LinkedIn. We respond within 30 days.
